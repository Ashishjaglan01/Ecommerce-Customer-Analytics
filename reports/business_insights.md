# Business Insights and Recommendations
## E-Commerce Customer Analytics

### Project Overview
This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product-category revenue, seller performance, and shipping costs. Power BI was used to create a three-page interactive dashboard covering sales overview, customer insights and retention, and product and seller performance.

### Major Findings
1. **Sales performance:** The dataset contains 99,441 orders and approximately R$16.01 million in total payment value. Monthly payment value generally increased during much of the observed period, although the final months contain too little activity for reliable trend conclusions.

2. **Customer retention:** The analysis identifies 96,096 unique customers, of whom 2,997 are classified as repeat customers under the current order-count definition. The 3.12% repeat-customer rate suggests an opportunity to investigate ways to encourage repeat purchases.

3. **Geographic concentration:** São Paulo has the largest customer base among the states displayed. This concentration can help guide regional marketing and customer-service planning.

4. **Product categories:** Health Beauty and Watches Gifts lead the displayed product-category revenue ranking. These categories merit further analysis of sales trends, product availability, and profitability.

5. **Seller performance:** The dashboard identifies the top 10 sellers by sales. Seller evaluation should also consider delivery performance, cancellations, and customer satisfaction.

6. **Shipping costs:** Average freight per order item is approximately R$19.99. The variation in freight costs suggests that shipment-level analysis could help identify unusually expensive deliveries.

### Recommendations
- Develop customer-retention experiments to encourage second purchases.
- Investigate late deliveries and compare performance across sellers and destinations.
- Monitor category-level revenue alongside order volume and profitability.
- Evaluate sellers using multiple performance indicators rather than sales alone.
- Investigate freight-cost outliers before making operational changes.
- Exclude or separately flag incomplete final-period data when reporting monthly trends.

### Limitations
The dataset covers a limited historical period, and September–October 2018 contain only 20 orders. Repeat-customer classification depends on the selected order-count definition. Revenue and payment value should not automatically be treated as profit, and freight outliers require investigation before being considered errors.

### Conclusion
The analysis demonstrates how an interactive business intelligence dashboard can convert raw e-commerce data into actionable questions about sales, retention, regional demand, seller performance, and logistics. The findings provide a foundation for further analysis of profitability, delivery reliability, and customer lifetime value.
